I am a master's student in Business Administration (Management Decision Science) at Ewha Womans University, advised by Prof. Yuncheol Kang in the AI Applications Lab (AAPL). I expect to graduate in August 2027.

I received my B.B.A. in Business Administration and B.S. in Statistics from Ewha Womans University. I also spent a semester as an exchange student at Bern University of Applied Sciences in Switzerland.

My interests lie in analytical operations management, stochastic modeling, and sequential decision-making. I enjoy understanding the structure of operational problems and developing models that help make better decisions.
